USGS-GEODAWN

U.S. Geological Survey, DOI 10.5066/P93LGLVQ

  • GeoDAWN includes airborne magnetic and radiometric surveys intended to support geothermal and critical-mineral studies.
  • Area 2 flight lines were spaced 400 m apart at an azimuth of 90 degrees; leveling residuals are a plausible non-fault magnetic-lineament mimic.

SCHREURS-2003

Geological Society of London publisher abstract

  • Distributed dextral-shear experiments develop left-stepping en echelon Riedel shears and short synthetic linking shears.
  • Later fault patterns are influenced by existing master faults and local stress changes.
abstract retrieved; full article is not claimed to have been reviewedReview source ↗
SAVAGE-BRODSKY-2011

AGU / Wiley primary paper

  • Fracture density decays away from the primary fault, and mature damage patterns can be represented by superposed contributions from secondary strands.
  • The displacement/zone-width relationship changes with fault maturity.
  • Mapped connected-component pixel count is only our noisy length proxy; the paper does not verify its suitability for this particular raster.
abstract and introduction retrievedReview source ↗
