1. The metric, and what it implies

Distance-weighted Tversky index, α = 0.2, β = 0.8, triangular kernel k(d) = max(1 − d/300 m, 0), i.e. 3 px on this 100 m grid. There are exactly 25 lattice offsets with non-zero kernel weight and only six distinct credit values: 1, 0.667, 0.529, 0.333, 0.255, 0.057.

Writing T for the covered truth credit (a sum over truth cells) and M for the dots' total self-credit (a sum over dots):

D   = alpha*(T + n - M) + beta*|G|
DTI = T / D

T and M are not the same quantity. T saturates at 1 per truth cell, M at 1 per dot, so a cluster of dots on one truth cell has M > T. Collapsing D to alpha*n + beta*|G| is only valid when T = M — an error in the shared template's prose, corrected here and pinned by tests/test_metric.py (IR-57-TPL-01).

Adding one dot with marginal truth credit dT and self-credit k raises DTI exactly when

dT > alpha * DTI * (dT + 1 - k)

which reduces to the familiar k > alpha * DTI only for a non-redundant dot. Since k = E[k](x) is fixed per candidate, this factorises into a per-candidate bar c * (1 − k) with c = alpha*DTI / (1 − alpha*DTI), which is what src/gems57/emit.py applies.

2. The holdout

Hide-and-recover, spatially blocked. The footprint is split into four quadrants at its median row and column; fold q withholds whole segments inside quadrant q and scores inside a 12 px eroded domain, so withheld strands are spatially separated from the catalogue context used to build the features. Only segments lying wholly inside the domain are eligible, so truth is never clipped. Every feature is rebuilt from the visible faults alone. Visible fault pixels are masked out of scoring pixel-exactly. Two draws (20, 21) give 8 cells.

Two withholding modes are reported:

  • all — the primary instrument.
  • detached — only segments belonging to a connected component that lies at least 4 px (400 m) from every other component. 896 of 3,199 components qualify (17,556 of 60,988 pixels, 28.8 %). These are the strands whose recovery does not benefit from a visible trace running into them, so this is the conservative reading and the closer analogue to a genuinely unmapped splay.

The canary fires on d and d_perp, and rule 4 requires that to be treated as leakage until proven otherwise. It is not label leakage — the features are computed from visible traces only and the labels are genuinely hidden pixels. It is an external-validity problem: withheld catalogue segments are physically attached to visible traces, so near-field distance is almost definitionally informative, whereas a genuinely uncatalogued splay need not be. The detached tables are the disproof experiment — they withhold only whole components sitting at least 4 px from every other component, breaking that attachment. See IR-57-CANARY-02 on the irregularities page.

Session 2 note: in session 1's table sin2/cos2 showed a discriminative AUC of exactly 0.5000 — that was the symptom of IR-57-STRIKE-01 (the encoding was fed a constant zero strike), not a property of the data. With the frame corrected they read 0.52–0.53, and the two new interaction features sin2d/cos2d screen at 0.60–0.64 — all clear of the 0.90 bar.

The null is the relative strike of visible trace pixels against their nearest visible pixel belonging to a different connected component. Comparing a visible pixel to its own nearest visible pixel returns itself and an angle of 0 — that was the first implementation's bug (IR-57-NULL-01).

Result: withheld strands are modestly more oblique than background cross-component pairs, with the excess at 15–45° (ratio 1.5–2.1), not at the textbook Riedel R-shear angle of 10–15°. This is precisely why the brief says not to hard-code textbook angles.

5. Allocation

p(x) is a gradient-boosted classifier over the nine anatomy features, calibrated to the observed withheld base rate by a single moment-matching scale. The expected credit of a dot is E[k] = p ⊛ k. Dots are then chosen by round-based greedy maximisation of the covered credit under the exact bar above. The budget is not chosen by hand: it is the per-draw dot total the holdout selected.

6. What is deliberately not claimed

The holdout truth is withheld catalogue pixels, which by construction belong to mapped systems. Genuinely unmapped faults are a different population, and their distance distribution is probably wider. The detached mode is the conservative reading of the same instrument. No live score is projected from any number on this page.
