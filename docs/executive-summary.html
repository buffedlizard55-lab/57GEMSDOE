Why zeros version fixes the portal error

DrivenData rejects submissions whose values fall outside [0,1], and also rejects rasters whose nodata tag is large negative sentinel -3.4028234663852886e+38 (used in training_features.tif, 7,113,308 cells, 3,061 inside footprint). Two failures produce same message:

  • Mechanism 1: float32 sentinel outside [0,1]
  • Mechanism 2: NaN sentinel with nodata=NaN — legitimate competition format but one validator change away from rejection.

Fix: Every GeoTIFF here is single-band float32, EPSG:32611, 100 m, 3730×3292, every one of 12,279,160 cells finite, min 0.0 max 1.0, zero cells outside [0,1], zero NaN, no nodata tag (zeros version) or nodata=NaN with NaN outside but finite inside (nan version).
