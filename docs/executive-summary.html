  1. Download the .tif from the button above (or the single-TIFF .zip — it contains exactly one GeoTIFF and nothing else). Do not upload this web page, a JSON receipt, a PDF, or a ZIP of the repository.
  2. Open the competition submission page. DrivenData: submissions. Log in, accept the official rules, and check the weekly counter shown on your own logged-in page. The published rules allow three submissions per week.
