Layers/data: Visible-only fault skeleton/branch graph, endpoint and junction class/distance, local strike, cross-/along-strike offset, and component length from the competition fault raster/vectors; Recorded INGENIOUS trace-segment sense (`sense`: N, RL, LL, or unknown) from data/external/trace_segments_utm11.csv, only after a predeclared visible-host join is shown reliable

Physical signature: Fit class-conditional distributions for withheld-strand location relative to the nearest visible tip or junction: distance, signed normalized along-strike/cross-strike offset, and relative strike. Estimate only on training folds, shrink sparse topology/sense classes to a pooled fit, and impose no textbook angle.

Why it may find missing faults: Splays and relay strands may cluster near visible branch terminations or junctions rather than uniformly along a broad halo. A fitted topology-conditioned neighborhood can allocate probability to off-trace geometry; recorded sense is only a possible stratifier, not a hard-coded stepping rule.

What differs from inspected work: The archived explore_zone.py/explore_zone2.py scripts measured d, relative azimuth, normalized along-strike position, length, and marginal sense-conditioned distance; the newer run_sense_experiment.py and run_orientation_experiments.py tested recorded-sense feature sets with existing geometry/orientation. The active feature matrix has no explicit visible branch-end/junction topology by fold. This is a narrower topology-conditioned refinement, not a new generic sense-by-angle/distance proposal or a claim of global novelty.

Prior work / distinction: An archived pre-merge slate (docs/research/hypotheses_h57.md) had already proposed generic tip-relay and junction/stepover ideas, but no result in that slate shows them implemented or run. The historical run_orientation_experiments.py also included sense with geometry/orientation; its recorded-sense comparison is HOLDOUT-DTI, evaluator gems57-pooled-hide-v2, 11,321 withheld positives, paired delta +0.003580783591493636, 95% CI [-0.005722027160876473, 0.014532120997528746], and sense was not retained. Do not present generic sense conditioning or a generic relay idea as novel; only this explicit visible-topology × sense-stratified fit remains untested in the inspected code.
