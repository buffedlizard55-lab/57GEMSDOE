What this file is

The organizers' hidden labels are new expert-mapped faults not in USGS/INGENIOUS, including splays and parallel strands of existing systems. The lane fits the anatomy of those secondary strands — radial distance from the host fault, azimuth relative to its strike, along-strike position, host length — measured on a hide-and-recover holdout (withheld catalogue segments, 300 m truth buffer, features rebuilt from the visible faults only), then emits the top 60,000 pixels of the fitted intensity, off every known USGS/INGENIOUS fault pixel.
