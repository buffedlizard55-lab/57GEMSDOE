Session 7 · H6-1 catalogue-proximity pruning vs matched controls

The owner-reported 0.2778 file is exactly its 0.2708 base pruned at 2 px from the mapped catalogue (file-measured). Session 7 tested that rule on the buffered whole-component holdout against both controls at matched dot counts. Result: negative, and not by noise.
